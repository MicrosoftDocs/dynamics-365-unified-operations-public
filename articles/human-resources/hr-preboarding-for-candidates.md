# Complete preboarding as a candidate (preview)

[!INCLUDE [banner](../includes/banner.md)]

[!include [Applies to Human Resources](../includes/applies-to-hr.md)]

[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-banner.md)]

After HR invites you to preboarding, provide your information on your new employer's careers site before your first day. You can work at your own pace, save your progress, and come back later. When you're ready, review everything and submit it to HR.

> [!NOTE]
> The steps and fields that you see depend on how your employer set up preboarding. Your employer can hide steps, make steps mandatory, and add their own fields.

## Prerequisites

- You received a preboarding invitation email from your new employer.
- You have a web browser on a computer, tablet, or phone.

### Start preboarding

When HR starts your preboarding, you receive an email. What you do next depends on whether you already have an account on your new employer's careers site.

### If you already have an account

If you used the careers site before, for example, to apply for this job, you don't need a new account or an invitation. Your email has a link to the careers site.

1. Open the email, and select the link in it.
1. Sign in with the account that you already use on the careers site.
1. In the menu at the top of the page, select **Preboarding (preview)**.

> [!NOTE]
> If you're already signed in to the careers site when HR starts your preboarding, **Preboarding (preview)** can take up to 5 minutes to appear in the menu. Wait a few minutes, and then refresh the page. 

### If you're new to the careers site

If you haven't used the careers site before, your email has an invitation link. You use it once, to create your account.

1. Open the invitation email, and select the link in it.
1. The careers site opens a registration page. Your email address is filled in from the invitation and can't be changed.
1. Create your account by using one of the sign-in options on the page, with the same email address that the invitation was sent to.
1. In the menu at the top of the page, select **Preboarding (preview)**.

### The Preboarding page

The **Preboarding** page shows your **Role**, **Hiring manager**, and **Start date**. It also shows:

- A card with your current stage and what to do next.
- What's happening next – A timeline of the stages after you submit: **Details and documents submitted**, **HR review details**, and **Pre-employment check**.
- Recommended resources – Links from your employer to help you settle in.

The card changes as you progress:

| You see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Get started with your preboarding** | HR invited you. | Select **Begin**. |
| **Action required** – **Submit details and documents** | You started, but didn't submit yet. | Select **Resume task**. |
| **Action required: Rework requested** | HR asked you to update some information. The card shows HR's instructions. | Select **Update and resubmit**. |
| **Reviewing details** | You submitted, and HR is reviewing your information. | Nothing. |
| **HR review approved** | HR approved your information. | Nothing. |
| **Pre-employment check in progress** | Your employer started your pre-employment check. | Nothing. |
| **All set, see you on your start date** or **Welcome aboard! See you on your start date** | Your preboarding is complete. | Nothing. |
| **Application closed** | Your employer isn't proceeding, or cancelled your preboarding. Select **View details** for more information. | Nothing. |

You also receive an email when your stage changes.

### Complete your preboarding 

1. On the **Preboarding** page, select **Begin** or **Resume task**.
1. The **Personal Information and Documents** page opens. The list of steps shows each step and its status: **Not started**, **In progress**, or **Completed**.
1. Complete each step, and then select **Next**. You can select any step in the list to go to it.
1. To stop and come back later, select **Save and close**.

In steps where you can add several items, such as addresses or dependents, select **+ Add New**, fill in the page, and then select **Save details**. Each item is saved when you select **Save details**. To change or remove an item, select **Edit** or **Delete** on it. If you leave a page without saving, you're asked whether to leave without saving.

The following steps can appear. Fields that are marked with an asterisk (\*) are required.

| Step | What you provide |
| --- | --- |
| Personal details | Your name, date of birth, nationality, and gender. Check your role, start date, hiring manager, and legal entity, and then select **I confirm the above role details are correct.** If something is wrong, contact your HR representative. |
| Address | Your addresses, with a description, purpose (**Current**, **Permanent**, or **Previous**), country/region, city, street, and zip code. |
| Contact details | Your phone numbers under **Phone details**, and your email addresses under **Email addresses**. Mark your primary phone number and email address. |
| Dependents | Your dependents, for benefits enrollment. A date of birth can't be in the future. Leave it empty if you have none. |
| Emergency contact | The people to contact in an emergency, with their relationship and phone number. |
| Identification | Your identification documents, such as a passport or driver's license, with their ID type, ID number, issuing authority, and expiry date. Select **Enter manually** to add each one. |
| Professional experience | Your previous employers, positions, and dates. |
| Education | Your education, starting with the discipline. |
| Skills | Your skills, with the rating and level. |
| Certifications | Your certifications, with their start and end dates. |
| Screenings | The screenings that your employer asks about. |
| Documentation | The documents that your employer asks for. Some are optional. |
| Review and finish | A summary of everything that you entered. |

Steps that don't apply to you show **Skip if not applicable.**

### Upload documents

In the **Documentation** step, each document that your employer asks for appears on its own card, with the accepted formats and the maximum size per file.

1. On the card for the document, select **Upload files**.
1. Select one or more files.

If a file isn't one of the accepted types, or is too large, it isn't uploaded, and a message tells you why. For security, web pages, SVG images, XML files, and scripts are never accepted, even if the name looks like another format, such as *CV.pdf.html*.

#### Review and submit

1. Go to the **Review and finish** step.
1. Check your information. To change a section, select **Edit** for that section.
1. Select **Submit**.
1. If a required step isn't complete, **Complete remaining steps** lists the steps to finish first.
1. In the **Consent for pre-employment check** dialog, read the text, and then select **I consent**.

   If you select **Deny**, your information isn't submitted. You can come back and submit at any time.

The message **Information submitted successfully** confirms that HR will review your details next. You also receive a confirmation email. Your information is read-only while HR reviews it.

### If your submission couldn't be saved

After you submit, your information is saved in your employer's HR system. Sometimes that process can fail, for example, because a value isn't accepted. If it fails:

- You receive an email that says your submission couldn't be saved, explains what went wrong, and gives a reference number.
- Your preboarding unlocks again, and you don't lose anything that you entered.
- HR is notified.

Sign in to the careers site, select **Preboarding**, correct the information that the email mentions, and submit again. If it keeps failing, contact your HR representative and quote the reference number from the email.

## Update your information when HR asks for rework

If HR needs changes, you receive an email, and the **Preboarding** page shows **Action Required: Rework requested** with HR's instructions.

1. Sign in to the careers site, and select **Preboarding**.
1. Read HR's instructions, and then select **Update and resubmit**.
1. The first step that HR reopened opens. You can edit the steps that HR reopened. The other steps stay read-only.
1. Update the information, and then go to **Review and finish**.
1. Select **Submit**, and give your consent again.

#### Get help

If you have questions about your preboarding, contact your HR representative or the hiring manager that's shown on the **Preboarding** page. Emails from preboarding come from an address that isn't monitored, so don't reply to them.
