# List of configurable business documents

[!include[banner](../includes/banner.md)]

This article provides the list of Electronic reporting (ER) configurations that are released in Dynamics 365 Finance to support configurable business documents.

The list of ER configurations for Finance is updated regularly. Open the [configuration repository of Microsoft configuration provider](er-download-configurations-global-repo.md) to review the list of ER configurations that are currently supported.
You can [filter](../../../finance/localizations/enhanced-filtering-global-repo.md) the repository to review the list of ER configurations that support configurable business documents.

:::image type="content" source="./media/bdm-overview-filterglobalrepo.gif" alt-text="Screenshot of filtering the content of the Global repository on the Configuration repository page.":::
