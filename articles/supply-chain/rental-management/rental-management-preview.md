# About the rental management preview (preview)

[!INCLUDE [banner](../includes/banner.md)]
[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-banner.md)]

Rental management is a set of capabilities in Dynamics 365 Finance and Supply Chain Management for organizations whose core business depends on renting physical assets. It covers the end-to-end rental lifecycle, from quoting and reservation through contract execution, inspection, and financial settlement, and it integrates with existing Dynamics 365 operational and financial processes. Maintenance of rental fleet items is supported through integration with Dynamics 365 Field Service.

[!INCLUDE [preview-note](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-note-d365.md)]

## About this preview

This public preview provides early access to rental management capabilities that are under active development. The objective is to validate rental scenarios more broadly, gather structured feedback from real-world usage, and use those insights to refine functionality, usability, and performance ahead of general availability.

The preview covers the essential rental processes:

- Fleet item master data, availability, and reservations.
- Rental quotations and contracts, including attachments and bulk fleet items.
- Shipping and receiving, including processing fleet item movements<!-- with the Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management mobile app-->.
- Utilization tracking, rate and discount management, and billing and invoicing.
- Financial integration with fixed assets and the general ledger.

Features described in these articles might change, be extended, or be removed based on feedback and ongoing development. Don't interpret these features as a commitment to future functionality or timelines.
