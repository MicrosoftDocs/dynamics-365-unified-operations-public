# View and confirm purchase orders in the supplier portal (preview)

[!include [banner](../includes/banner.md)]

[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-banner.md)]

The **Purchase order confirmation** workspace in the supplier portal lets vendors review, confirm, or respond to purchase orders (POs) that purchasers send. The portal provides a workspace where vendors can access all relevant POs, check their status, and take appropriate actions such as accepting, rejecting, or suggesting changes.

To open the **Purchase order confirmation** workspace, sign in to the supplier portal and complete any of the following steps:

- On the top navigation bar, select **Purchase orders** and then select any of the views listed.
- On the home page, select any of the links in the **Purchase order confirmation** panel.

The workspace displays tiles for the following statuses:

- **For Review** – POs that require a vendor response (unanswered or edited).
- **Awaiting Customer Action** – POs that await purchaser action after vendor response.
- **Pending delivery** – POs that the purchaser confirmed but aren't yet fully received.

Each tile shows the number of POs in that category and provides a link to view the related list of POs. Select a tile to view the list of POs in that status. From the list, you can open a PO to review its details and communicate with the purchaser as needed.
