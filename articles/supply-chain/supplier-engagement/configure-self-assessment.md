# Configure self-assessment questions (preview)

[!include [banner](../includes/banner.md)]

[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-banner.md)]

Vendors see self-assessment questions in the supplier portal during onboarding. These questions help you evaluate vendor compliance and capabilities before you make qualification decisions. A well-structured question set also makes assessments more consistent across vendors.

## Create, delete, and edit self-assessment questions

Use the following procedure to manage self-assessment questions in the Supplier Engagement app.

1. Open the Supplier Engagement app, and at the bottom of the navigation pane, select the **Configuration** area.
1. On the navigation pane, go to **Self-assessment** \> **Questions**.
1. Either open an existing record to edit it or, on the command bar, select **New** to create a record. You can also select **Delete** to remove a selected record.
1. Enter the following information for the new or selected record:
    - **Name** – Enter the question exactly as you want potential vendors to see it.
    - **Number** – Control the order in which questions appear on the page. Questions with lower numbers appear first. If two questions have the same number, they're shown in creation order. To keep sorting consistent, use leading zeros for single-digit values, such as 01, 02, 03, and 10.
    - **Area** – Select the operational area that the question applies to. Available areas include *Supply Chain Management*, *Quality management*, *Quality control*, *Human resources*, *Environmental*, *Social*, and *Governance (ESG)*, and *Production*.
    - **Answer type** – Choose whether vendors respond by entering **Text** or selecting **Yes/No**.
1. Select **Save**.
