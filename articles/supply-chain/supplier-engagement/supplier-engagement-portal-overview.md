# Supplier portal overview (preview)

[!include [banner](../includes/banner.md)]

[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-banner.md)]

The supplier portal provides an external-facing web experience where vendors can interact with your organization's procurement processes. Built on Microsoft Power Pages, the portal gives vendors self-service access to manage their profiles, respond to requests for quotation (RFQs), confirm purchase orders, submit invoices, and track consignment inventory—all without needing direct access to Supply Chain Management.

This article provides an overview of the portal's capabilities from the vendor's perspective. The information here is intended for administrators and Microsoft partners who need to understand what vendors can do in the portal and where customization opportunities exist.

Learn more about the supplier portal and how it works with the other components of Supplier Engagement in [Supplier Engagement overview](supplier-engagement-overview.md).

## Portal capabilities

The supplier portal supports the following vendor workflows:

- **Landing page and onboarding** – Vendors can register as new suppliers and complete onboarding steps to provide company information, capabilities, and certifications. Learn more in [Landing page and vendor onboarding](supplier-engagement-portal-landing.md).
- **Home page** – After signing in, vendors see a personalized home page with workspace tiles, essential links, and notifications. Learn more in [Home page](supplier-engagement-portal-home-page.md).
- **User profile** – Vendors can view and edit their personal profile information. Learn more in [User profile](supplier-engagement-portal-user-profile.md).
- **RFQ responses** – Vendors can view, bid on, and manage requests for quotation. Learn more in [View and respond to RFQs](supplier-engagement-portal-request-quotation.md).
- **Purchase order confirmation** – Vendors can review, confirm, or respond to purchase orders. Learn more in [View and confirm purchase orders](supplier-engagement-portal-purchase-orders.md).
- **Invoicing** – Vendors can create, submit, and track invoices. Learn more in [View and create invoices](supplier-engagement-portal-invoices.md).
- **Consignment inventory** – Vendors can monitor consignment stock levels and consumption. Learn more in [View consignment details](supplier-engagement-portal-consignment.md).
- **Global and local vendor data** – Both global and local vendors can manage their organizational profile, contacts, certificates, and capabilities. Learn more in [Vendor details](supplier-engagement-portal-vendor-details.md).
- **User access management** – Vendor administrators can manage portal users, assign roles, and control access. Learn more in [Vendor details](supplier-engagement-portal-vendor-details.md).
