# Work with the Supplier Engagement app (preview)

[!include [banner](../includes/banner.md)]

[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-banner.md)]
<!-- KFM: Preview until further notice -->

The Supplier Engagement app is a model-driven app for internal users who manage supplier relationships. It's built on Microsoft Dataverse. It gives teams a single place to review supplier registrations, maintain shared vendor data, and coordinate legal entity–specific setups. Changes that you make in the app sync automatically with Supply Chain Management. The app also works with the supplier portal to support vendor self-service.

## Supplier Engagement app overview

The Supplier Engagement app organizes supplier work into the following areas:

- **Review and approve vendor registrations** – Process incoming supplier registration requests, review submitted details, and approve or reject requests to start onboarding.
- **Manage global vendors** – Create and maintain global vendor records that act as master profiles across legal entities. You can track vendor information, capabilities, certificates, contacts, lifecycle status, feedback, risks, and activities.
- **Manage local vendors** – Release global vendors to specific legal entities as local vendor accounts, update local vendor details, add contacts and bank accounts, and manage localization-specific data.
- **Vendor holds** – Put global or local vendors on hold to restrict invoices, payments, requisitions, or all transactions, and release the hold when it's no longer needed.
- **Dashboards** – Use role-based dashboards to monitor activities, registration pipelines, risk insights, vendor distributions, and portal access status.

Because the app is connected to Supply Chain Management and the supplier portal, users can work across onboarding and ongoing vendor management without reentering the same information. Global vendor data stays centralized in Dataverse, while transactional readiness and legal entity release stay aligned with Supply Chain Management.
