# Manage activities (preview)

[!include [banner](../includes/banner.md)]

[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-banner.md)]

The **Activities** page provides a central place to track interactions, follow-up work, and scheduled events in the Supplier Engagement app. Built-in views help teams monitor open work, recent activities, due dates, and portal-facing timeline items. Users can create tasks, appointments, and other activity types directly from the **Activities** page.

## Open the activities page

To open the **Activities** page:

1. Open the Supplier Engagement app, and at the bottom of the navigation pane, select the **Menu** area.
1. On the navigation pane, go to **General** \> **Activities**.
1. On the **Activities** page, use the drop-down list at the top of the page to choose the types of activities to show. The default view is **My Activities**, which shows activities that are assigned to you. You can also use the search bar to filter activities by keyword, and you can sort the list by selecting the column headers.

## Add a new activity

To add a new activity, follow these steps:

1. Open the **Activities** page.
1. On the command bar, select the button for the type of activity that you want to create. For example, create a **Task** when you need to track a work item or follow-up action. Create an **Appointment** when you need to schedule a meeting, review, or other time-based event.
1. Fill out the details for your activity.
1. On the command bar, select **Save & close**.

## Work with activities

To work with your assigned activities, follow these steps:

1. Open the **Activities** page.
1. Use the view selector and other filters to find the activities you need.
1. Either select the link in the Subject column to open the activity record, or select the check box for one or more activities.
1. Use the buttons on the command bar to take action. For example, select **Mark as complete** to close an activity, select **Assign** to assign the activity to another user, or select **Delete** to remove an activity. If you opened an activity record, you can also edit the details and select **Save & close** to save your changes.

The command bar also includes the following buttons for working with activities:

- **Focused view** – Opens a view that makes it easy to browse records using a list pane and a details pane. Select a record in the list to view its details. Use the details pane to edit and take action on the record. Select the **Read only grid** button to go back to the standard view.
- **Show chart** – Opens a chart view where you can choose various graphical representations of your and your team's activities, their statuses, completion rates, and so on.

## Dashboard views

The **Activities** page includes the following dashboard views.

- **All Activities** – Displays all activity records, regardless of status or owner.
- **Closed Activities** – Shows activities that are completed or closed.
- **Due and Overdue Activities** – Lists activities that are due soon or past the due date.
- **Last Activity** – Displays the most recent activity for each record.
- **My Activities** – Shows activities that are assigned to you. This is the default view.
- **My Closed Activities** – Lists your closed activities.
- **My Team Members' Activities** – Displays activities for your team members.
- **Next Activity** – Shows the next scheduled activity for each record.
- **Open Activities** – Lists activities that are currently open.
- **Portal Timeline View** – Shows a timeline of activities that are visible in the external portal.
- **Scheduled Activities** – Displays activities that are scheduled for a future date.
- **All Tasks** – Shows all task records.
- **My Tasks** – Displays tasks that are assigned to you.
- **All Appointments** – Shows all appointment records.
- **My Appointments** – Displays appointments that are assigned to you.
- **My Completed Appointments** – Lists your completed appointments.
- **My Draft Appointments** – Shows draft appointments that you created.
