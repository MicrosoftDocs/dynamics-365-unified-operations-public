# Global vendor feedback (preview)

[!include [banner](../includes/banner.md)]

[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-banner.md)]
<!-- KFM: Preview until further notice -->

Global vendor feedback provides a standardized way to evaluate suppliers and capture performance insights in one place. Ratings are stored centrally and contribute to a dynamic overall score. As you add new feedback, the overall rating updates automatically.

## Enter global vendor feedback

Create a feedback entry when you want to record a new supplier evaluation for a global vendor.

1. [Open the relevant global vendor record](supplier-engagement-global-vendor-info.md#open-a-global-vendor-record).
1. On the command bar, select **Feedback**.
1. On the feedback form, enter a **Title**.
1. Enter a rating (1–5 stars) for each factor:
    - **Sales policy and flexibility** – How well the supplier supports commercial terms, negotiations, and flexibility in doing business.
    - **Communication** – Responsiveness, clarity, and consistency in supplier communication.
    - **Competitiveness** – Pricing strength and commercial competitiveness.
    - **Product offering and innovation** – Value, breadth, and innovation of the supplier's products or services.

1. Enter a **Comment**.
1. Select **Save and close**.

## View feedback

You can submit multiple feedback entries for the same vendor. As you add more entries, the overall rating adjusts automatically. The system calculates each global vendor's overall feedback score as the average of all the submitted rating factors. To view a global vendor's feedback history, [open the vendor record](supplier-engagement-global-vendor-info.md#open-a-global-vendor-record), go to the **General** tab, and look in the **Feedback** section.

## Feedback score in Supply Chain Management

The latest feedback score syncs to Supply Chain Management, where it is also shown on the global vendor record. This shared visibility lets you review supplier performance from either application.
