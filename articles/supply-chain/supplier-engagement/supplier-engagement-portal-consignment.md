# View consignment details in the supplier portal (preview)

[!include [banner](../includes/banner.md)]

[!INCLUDE [preview-banner](~/../shared-content/shared/preview-includes/preview-banner.md)]

The supplier portal lets vendors view information about the stock level of products that they place in consignment with a customer. Vendors can also monitor consumption of their stock when the customer takes ownership of the inventory.

## Work with the Consignment inventory workspace

The **Consignment inventory** workspace helps vendors track and manage consignment inventory. To open it, sign in to the supplier portal and complete any of the following steps:

- On the top navigation bar, select **Consignment inventory** and then select any of the views listed.
- On the home page, select any of the links in the **Consignment inventory** panel.

The workspace displays a set of tiles, each of which represents a status of consignment inventory. Each tile displays a count of items in that status. Select a tile to view a list of consignment inventory items in that status. The following tiles are available in the **Consignment inventory** workspace:

- **On-hand** – View the current stock owned by the vendor at the customer's warehouse. The page lists inventory owned by the vendor that is stored at the purchaser's warehouse.
- **Received items** – Track the inventory that the customer consumed after the inventory ownership change journal was posted. The page lists product receipt lines that were generated when ownership of the consignment inventory changed from the vendor to the customer. Select a purchase order number to view the related purchase order.
- **Consigned POs** – View purchase orders generated when consignment inventory is consumed and received. Select a purchase order number to view its details.
- **Invoiced POs** – View consignment purchase orders that were invoiced. Select a purchase order number to view header and line details, including consignment-inventory-specific fields.
