# Trade point of sale in Dynamics 365 Commerce

[!INCLUDE [banner](../includes/banner.md)]

Trade point of sale (Trade POS) gives sales associates a focused Store Commerce experience for fast, accurate sales at high-volume trade counters, wholesale locations, distributor branches, and business-to-business (B2B) service desks. It brings together capabilities for large carts, customer orders, inventory and delivery commitments, pricing and margin decisions, customer contacts, and business-ready receipts and documents.

The capabilities released in Dynamics 365 Commerce versions 10.0.49 and 10.0.50 reduce repetitive steps and help associates complete complex transactions without moving between separate workflows. The following sections provide a central index to the documentation for these capabilities.
