Dynamics 365 Commerce offers the following Copilot experiences.

| Feature | Description | Learn more |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| **Customer insights by Copilot** | Store associates can use Microsoft Copilot to enhance customer interactions and create personalized shopping experiences in Dynamics 365 Commerce. | [Customer insights by Copilot](/dynamics365/commerce/copilot-pos-customer-insights) |
| **Product insights by Copilot** |  Store associates can use Microsoft Copilot-generated product insights to quickly understand product information and deliver informed shopping assistance in the Microsoft Dynamics 365 Commerce Store Commerce app. | [Product insights by Copilot](/dynamics365/commerce/copilot-product-insights) |
| **Store report insights by Copilot** | This feature uses AI to generate natural language summaries of store reports in the Dynamics 365 Commerce Store Commerce app. | [Store report insights by Copilot](/dynamics365/commerce/copilot-pos-report-insights) |
| **Copilot AI-generated summaries that are relevant to statement posting on two pages: the Store financials workspace page and the Statements page.** | The Enable Copilot based summary and insights for Calculated and Posted statements feature uses Copilot to offer a comprehensive summary of insights that are derived from posted and unposted statements and transactions from the last seven days. The summary has two sections: Status and Risks. | [Enable Copilot statement posting summaries](/dynamics365/commerce/copilot-statement-summaries) |
| **Use Copilot in site builder to enrich product detail pages** | Copilot in site builder works seamlessly with Dynamics 365 Commerce site builder to help you quickly create product enrichment and marketing content customized for your target audience and brand tone. | [Use Copilot in site builder to enrich product detail pages](/dynamics365/commerce/copilot-site-builder) |
| **Streamline your merchandising process with Copilot-based insights** | Copilot can help you get a comprehensive summary of insights for the merchandising configurations of your retail channels. Use this summary of insights to simplify your merchandising workflows in Dynamics 365 Commerce. | [Streamline your merchandising process with Copilot-based insights](/dynamics365/commerce/copilot-based-merch-insights) |
